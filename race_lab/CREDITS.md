# Reused racer assets

These browser assets are exports of the existing Blender cast, not newly generated
faces. Original scenes and downloaded source images remain unchanged. Runtime
horse coat textures were baked from their Blender shaders; the photos were
embedded as JPEG textures for the browser export.

This is a local cutout-parody prototype; assets retain their individual terms.

photograph; no photographic expression or voice was generated.

The original rider dialogue is not included or retimed in this runtime.

## Race sounds

Runtime audio uses the U.S. Army bugle, a quiet outdoor audience and short
applause from stephan, gallop texture credited to Bruno Auzet / Freesound
Community, and original starter, hoof and comic power sound design. The twelve
comic launch/impact clips are original CC0-1.0 WAV files, with no sampled rider
speech or celebrity voice imitation. The old F1 air-horn track is not used.
[Full sources, licenses and adaptation notes](audio/CREDITS.md).
