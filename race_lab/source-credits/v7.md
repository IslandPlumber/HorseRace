# Gordon's angry-chef cutout

## Gordon Ramsay source image

- [Chef Gordon Ramsay template on Imgflip](https://imgflip.com/meme/Chef-Gordon-Ramsay).
- [Uncaptioned image downloaded](https://i.imgflip.com/115ah.jpg), retained unchanged
  at `sources/gordon_angry_chef.jpg` (468 × 498).
- The source image visibly carries a **20thC.Fox / Everett / Rex Features** credit.
  No photographer name beyond that credit has been verified.
- This is a third-party copyrighted image used for the local cutout parody
  prototype. No public-domain or open redistribution license is asserted.

Gordon's angry, open-mouth expression comes from that actual source image.
Only the head silhouette is traced into Blender geometry; UV coordinates address
the unchanged JPEG. The face is not generated or retouched. The separate white
double-breasted chef jacket, pointing finger, open palm and scolding motion are
puppet geometry and animation made for this scene. The photograph is not footage
of a horse race. No Gordon voice recording or synthetic speech is included.
