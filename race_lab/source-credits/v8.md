# Snoop's equestrian cutout

## Source photograph

- Photographer: **Mike Egerton / PA Images via Getty Images**.
- Snoop Dogg in formal riding clothes at the Paris Olympics, August 3, 2024.
- [NBC Olympics article and credit](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/our-favorite-snoop-dogg-looks-2024-paris-olympics).
- [Downloaded photograph](https://images.nbcolympics.com/sites/default/files/2024-08/489x725_snoop%20dogg_GettyImages_240803.jpg),
  retained unchanged at `sources/snoop_equestrian_getty_20240803.jpg` (489 × 725).
- Third-party copyrighted photograph. No public-domain or open redistribution
  license is asserted for this image.

The head silhouette includes the actual riding helmet and sunglasses. A separate
silhouette shows the actual formal coat, white shirt and gloved hands. Both are
UV-mapped Blender meshes addressing the unchanged source JPEG. The photographed
expression and clothes are not generated or retouched. White riding trousers,
black boots, the purple saddle blanket and exaggerated horse steps complete this
local cutout parody. No Snoop voice recording or speaking-mouth animation is added.
