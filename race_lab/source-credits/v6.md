The photographic cutout adaptation (head silhouette/UV mapping, enlargement and
animation) is also provided under **CC BY-SA 3.0**. Changes: the head silhouette is
traced with Blender mesh geometry, mapped to the original photograph, enlarged
and attached to a black T-shirt puppet. Photographic pixels are not generated,
retouched, or used for a synthetic voice. The author and Royal Society do not
endorse this prototype. Other scene assets retain their separate terms.

The black T-shirt, raised fists, electric-blue saddle blanket and enthusiastic
bouncing are stylized puppet geometry and animation made for this parody. They
are not a claim that the photograph depicts an actual horse-racing event. Elon
has no added voice recording or speaking-mouth animation.
