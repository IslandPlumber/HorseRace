# Reused racer assets

These browser assets are exports of the existing Blender cast, not newly generated
faces. Original scenes and downloaded source images remain unchanged. Runtime
horse coat textures were baked from their Blender shaders; the photos were
embedded as JPEG textures for the browser export.

No endorsement by the pictured people, photographers or organizations is implied.
This is a local cutout-parody prototype; assets retain their individual terms.
Elon's head silhouette, enlargement and animation are adaptations of the credited
photograph; no photographic expression or voice was generated.

Runtime: Three.js (MIT). Development tools: esbuild (MIT), Playwright (Apache-2.0).
The browser test is silent; it does not include or retime the previous dialogue.
