# A 12-rider comedy field

The target is **12**, with a mix of politicians and celebrities. There is no universal maximum across horse racing: the [Breeders’ Cup](https://members.breederscup.com/Championships/FieldSelection) uses limits of 12 or 14, and the [Kentucky Derby](https://www.kentuckyderby.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/08/Field-Size-2026.pdf) uses 20. Twelve gives this game a substantial field while keeping photo faces readable.

The first four are built. The remaining eight are casting recommendations; their faces, voices and special animations have not been built or sourced yet.

| Rider | Visual joke | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Donald Trump | Throws the red cap and says the actual “You’re fired!” recording. | Built |
| Joe Biden | Ducks the cap and answers with his real “C’mon, man!” clip. | Built |
| Mitch McConnell | His blank stare never changes, however absurd the race gets. | Built |
| Bernie Sanders | Parka and mittens, arms folded; lets the horse do all the work. | Built |
| Snoop Dogg | Proper riding clothes, utterly relaxed while his horse does a ridiculous dressage step. | Proposed |
| Elon Musk | Bounces enthusiastically in the saddle while the horse stays serious. | Proposed |
| Gordon Ramsay | Turns forward to yell at his own horse. | Proposed |
| Nicolas Cage | Huge wide-eyed reaction whenever someone passes him. | Proposed |
| Danny DeVito | Tiny stiff torso sitting low in the saddle, enormous photo head. | Proposed |
| Mike Tyson | Gloves up, shadowboxing the air while his horse carries on. | Proposed |
| Kamala Harris | Laughing reaction to the hat chaos, using a real recording if sourced. | Proposed |
| Boris Johnson | Wild hair and a bewildered face, as though he boarded the wrong ride. | Proposed |

**Strongest next additions:** Snoop for calm contrast, Gordon for anger, and Elon for excessive movement. Giving each rider one distinct joke should read better than having twelve talking heads all doing the same thing.

Snoop’s actual equestrian look is documented by [NBC Olympics](https://www.nbcolympics.com/news/our-favorite-snoop-dogg-looks-2024-paris-olympics). Elon’s jumping appearance is documented in [AP’s Butler photo coverage](https://apnews.com/article/beca49008e09cdc86c5685643b614a5f). The other proposed gags are creative directions, not claims about recorded events.

`field_12_capacity.png` is a layout test containing **12 actual horse meshes, four finished riders and eight numbered open seats**. Its camera reserves full-height head space over every saddle. It does not represent twelve completed characters. The current playable sample remains the four-rider video.
